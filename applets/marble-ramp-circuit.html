Marble Ramp Circuit v2

Each ramp is a piece of wire. Marbles are free electrons and the fixed balls are atoms. The potential difference across a ramp is the difference in height between its two ends. Dashed level lines run from each end to a ruler marked in volts, and the voltmeter is connected across both ends. Ammeters count electrons.
