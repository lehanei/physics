    Charge density

    σ = Q / A

    Charge density is the charge per unit area. Units: C / m², or C m⁻².

    Just outside a conductor, E = σ / ε. σ is above the line: as σ gets bigger, E gets bigger. So the field is strongest at the point, where the charge is most crowded.
