What the hand sends

The hand keeps shaking until you press Stop. Change f or the medium while it runs and watch λ change. Choose "No reflection" for a clean travelling wave; a fixed or free end sets up a standing wave.

λ = v/f. The speed is set by the slinky, so as f goes up, λ goes down: the pulses arrive closer together.

The medium: change the slinky

T is above the line, so as T increases, v increases. μ is below the line and under the root, so as μ increases, v decreases (v ∝ 1/√μ). Thicker or denser wire means more mass per metre.
